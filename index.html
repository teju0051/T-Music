Client Intelligence

Frequent Inquiries.

Everything you need to know about booking, our proprietary ecosystem, and studio policies.

01 Do you offer video production alongside audio?

Yes, through our official partnership with Zenteku Films, we can shoot, grade, and edit cinematic visuals for your tracks simultaneously. This ensures perfect synchronization between your audio and visual vision.

02 Do you support Dolby Atmos spatial mixing?

Yes. Our control rooms are fully calibrated for 7.1.4 immersive audio. We provide streaming-ready Dolby Atmos ADM BWF masters that meet the strict loudness and spatial criteria required by major platforms.

03 What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Please note that all bookings, deposits, and service payments at TaalKosha are strictly final. Refunds will not be issued under any circumstances. If you need to reschedule a session, you must contact our management at least 48 hours in advance.

04 Are there any age restrictions to use your services?

Yes. You must be at least 13 years of age to book studio time, distribute music, or use any of our interconnected services. Any creator under the age of 18 must be accompanied by a legal parent or guardian during physical studio sessions.

05 Do you provide session musicians?

Yes, our A&R team maintains a roster of elite session vocalists, guitarists, strings, and percussionists. This service must be requested during your pre-production consultation so we can arrange talent availability.

06 Can I bring my own engineer?

Yes, dry hire is available for established producers and engineers. However, a TaalKosha assistant engineer will always be present to ensure hardware safety and seamless integration with our patch bays.

07 Do you offer remote mixing and mastering?

We do. You can upload your raw stems via our secure client portal. Our engineers will run them through our analog outboard gear and deliver high-fidelity masters digitally, anywhere in the world.

08 What is your policy on file storage?

We securely backup all session files on our encrypted internal servers for 30 days after project completion. We highly recommend bringing a personal external drive to take a physical copy home with you immediately after your session.

09 Can I book a pre-session tour of the facility?

Absolutely. We encourage prospective clients to schedule a brief walk-through of Studio A and B to get a feel for the acoustic treatment and workflow before committing to a block booking.

10 How many revisions are included in a standard mix?

Standard mix packages include two comprehensive revision rounds. Further revisions or stem exports will be billed at our standard hourly studio rate.

11 Can my music be placed on UUUNIVVR?

Yes, but with specific conditions. Because UUUNIVVR is an OTT platform, standard audio-only distribution is not permitted. To distribute your music via this platform, you must either create an accompanying film or collaborate with a film production house.

Note: Only film production houses that are officially partnered under the UUUNIVVR platform ecosystem will be accepted for these releases.

12 How does 5onam AI integrate into my session?

5onam AI is a proprietary intelligence model developed by Zen-Tech AI. Operating on par with top-tier generative models, it assists our engineers in real-time acoustic analysis, frequency balancing, and workflow automation without compromising the human element of your art.

13 Do I retain 100% of my copyrights?

Yes. TaalKosha acts strictly as a service provider (unless a separate co-publishing or label deal is explicitly signed). You retain complete ownership of your intellectual property and master recordings.

14 What DAWs (Digital Audio Workstations) are available?

Our primary rigs run Pro Tools Ultimate for tracking and mixing. However, we also have fully updated licenses for Logic Pro X, Ableton Live 11, and FL Studio to accommodate all production workflows seamlessly.

15 Is there a lounge area for my entourage?

Yes, our facility features a private green room equipped with Wi-Fi, gaming consoles, and refreshments so your team can relax without interrupting the critical listening environment in the control room.

16 Do you provide analog mastering or just digital?

Both. Our mastering chain features a hybrid approach. We run your mix through high-end analog compressors and EQs for warmth and saturation, followed by digital limiters for maximum competitive loudness.

17 Can I book a session strictly for podcasting?

Yes, our vocal booths are perfect for high-fidelity voiceover work, audiobooks, and multi-host podcasts. We can even provide multi-cam video setups via Zenteku Films.

18 Are your studios suitable for cinematic scoring?

Absolutely. We specialize in Foley recording, ADR, and full cinematic scoring. The control rooms have massive visual monitors synced directly to the DAW for frame-accurate musical cues.

19 Do you distribute to Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube?

While our primary pipeline prioritizes our native UUUNIVVR platform, our label services division can handle global digital distribution to all major DSPs upon request.

20 Is your studio accessible 24/7?

We operate on an advanced booking schedule. "Lockout" sessions (which give you exclusive access to a room for 24 hours a day over multiple days) are available for intensive album creation projects.
